Why I built it this way
Classify before you retrieve
Most RAG apps send every query to the same path. Ask about your running habit — get back cooking journal entries. The AI hallucinates because it's searching the wrong data. The classifier routes habit questions to SQL, journal questions to vectors. The routing decision happens before retrieval, not after.
Evaluate every response, not just generate it
Generating an answer and trusting it are different things. GROUNDED means every sentence in this response traces back to your actual data. Faithfulness 100 means no hallucination. The 38 Context Precision is honest — the retrieval pulled more context than necessary. That's a known tradeoff I made for recall.
The context that compounds
Four layers of context assemble every response — your profile, recent entries, semantically retrieved history, and conversation turns. The longer you use Mindstream, the more Layer 4 knows. That's the moat. Not the features. The accumulated understanding.
